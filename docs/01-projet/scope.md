# AI Daily — Project Scope

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the current scope of AI Daily and establishes the boundaries of the project.

The scope is expected to evolve as the project develops. Changes should be documented when they significantly affect the product direction or planned features.

## 2. Initial Product Scope

The initial version of AI Daily is focused on helping users discover and understand relevant Artificial Intelligence news.

The initial product should include:

- A feed of AI-related news.
- News organized into relevant categories.
- A dedicated page for each news item.
- A concise summary of each news item.
- Basic contextual information to help users understand the news.
- Access to the original source.
- Basic search functionality.
- A responsive and mobile-first interface.
- Progressive Web App capabilities.

## 3. Content Scope

AI Daily may cover topics such as:

- Artificial Intelligence models.
- Machine Learning.
- Generative AI.
- AI tools and platforms.
- AI research.
- AI companies and products.
- Major developments in the AI ecosystem.

The project should prioritize relevant developments rather than attempting to collect every AI-related publication.

## 4. Initial Technical Scope

The project will progressively cover:

- Frontend application.
- Backend API.
- Database.
- News ingestion and processing pipeline.
- AI-assisted content processing.
- PWA capabilities.
- Automated testing.
- Deployment.

Technical choices may evolve as the project develops.

## 5. Future Scope

The following features may be considered after the initial version:

- Personalized news feeds.
- Content recommendations.
- Notifications.
- AI-generated explanations of technical concepts.
- Trend analysis.
- Comparison of multiple sources covering the same event.
- Advanced filtering.
- User accounts and preferences.
- Developer-oriented insights explaining why a news item may matter to developers.

These features are not part of the initial scope unless explicitly added later.

## 6. Out of Scope

AI Daily is not intended to become:

- A general-purpose social network.
- A replacement for original news sources.
- A general-purpose search engine.
- A platform for publishing unrestricted user-generated news.
- A general-purpose AI chatbot.
- A platform attempting to cover every technology topic.

Keeping these boundaries helps maintain a clear product direction.

## 7. Scope Evolution

The scope of AI Daily may change between versions.

Significant changes should be documented and associated with a specific project version or milestone.

The scope should always remain aligned with the project's vision, goals, and available development resources.
