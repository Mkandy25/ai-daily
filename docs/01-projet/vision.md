# AI Daily — Project Vision

## 1. Overview

AI Daily is a Progressive Web App (PWA) dedicated to tracking and understanding the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence.

The application aims to aggregate news from multiple sources and present them in a clear, concise, and accessible way.

AI Daily is designed for anyone interested in Artificial Intelligence, from curious users discovering the field to developers and enthusiasts who want to stay informed about new technologies, models, tools, research, and major developments.

---

## 2. Problem

The Artificial Intelligence landscape is evolving rapidly, with news and information coming from many different sources, including technology companies, researchers, developers, specialized media, and scientific publications.

This fragmentation makes it difficult to consistently keep up with the latest developments.

Furthermore, some AI-related news can be highly technical and difficult to understand for people without extensive knowledge of the field.

AI Daily aims to address two main challenges:

* the fragmentation of AI-related information;
* the difficulty of quickly understanding certain AI developments.

---

## 3. Vision

AI Daily aims to become a simple and accessible platform that allows anyone to follow and understand important developments in Artificial Intelligence without having to constantly consult multiple sources.

The application will prioritize clarity, concise information, and context rather than simply accumulating large amounts of content.

---

## 4. Value Proposition

AI Daily enables users to:

* discover important AI-related news;
* quickly understand the key information behind each story;
* access information aggregated from multiple sources;
* reduce the time required to keep up with developments in the field;
* explore the original source for further information.

The goal is therefore not simply to provide news, but to make AI-related information easier to understand.

---

## 5. Target Users

### 5.1 Curious Users

People who want to discover what is happening in the field of Artificial Intelligence without necessarily having advanced technical knowledge.

### 5.2 Developers and AI Enthusiasts

People who want to keep up with new models, tools, frameworks, research, products, and major developments in the AI ecosystem.

---

## 6. Product Principles

AI Daily will be guided by the following principles:

### Clarity

Information should be easy to understand and presented in a structured way.

### Conciseness

Users should be able to understand the essential information behind a news story quickly.

### Relevance

The application should prioritize genuinely useful and interesting information rather than simply increasing the amount of content available.

### Transparency

Every piece of information should be traceable to its original source whenever possible.

### Simplicity

The interface should remain simple, intuitive, and enjoyable to use, particularly on mobile devices.

---

## 7. Project Goals

AI Daily is also a personal learning and software engineering project.

Developing the project will provide hands-on experience with:

* frontend development;
* backend development;
* REST API design;
* databases;
* Artificial Intelligence service integration;
* software architecture;
* Progressive Web App development;
* testing;
* security;
* deployment;
* software documentation.

The project will be developed incrementally, with an emphasis on understanding technologies and architectural decisions rather than simply accumulating features.

---

## 8. Long-Term Vision

In the long term, AI Daily may evolve from a simple news aggregator into a platform designed to help users better understand the Artificial Intelligence ecosystem.

Potential features may include personalized content, recommendations, trend analysis, technical concept explanations, and comparisons between different sources.

These features are not necessarily part of the first version of the product and will be evaluated as the project evolves.
